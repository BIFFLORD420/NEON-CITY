Neon City Drive

Neon City sits on the coast in the south of a whole region: beaches, a marina, the port, the airport, a casino strip and an amusement pier by the sea; farms, small towns and a river inland; forested mountains, Lake Tahona and an observatory in the north. Leave town on I-5 (north), I-10 (east) or the Coast Highway. Race a 5 mile course through 10 checkpoints in the city.

Touch controls: arrows bottom-left to steer (slide your thumb between them), GAS and BRAKE bottom-right (hold BRAKE to reverse). DRIFT, OUT/IN, GARAGE, VIEW, MAP and RACE along the bottom. Pause is top-right. Best played sideways.

  • W / Up: accelerate   S / Down: brake and reverse
  • A D / Left Right: steer   Space: handbrake drift
  • R: start the race   C: paint   1 2 3: swap car   H: home
  • G: garage door   V: cockpit or chase view   O: showroom view   X: see-through body
  • Q E: indicators   Z: wipers   T: rain
  • F: get out / in   On foot: WASD, Shift run, Space jump, Q E elevator down / up
  • Drive onto a yellow MODS pad to paint (C) or swap cars (1 2 3)
  • Police patrol the city: speed (over 45 mph), run a red light or hit a police car near one and they chase you. Lose them, or stop to take a ticket
  • K: police encounter (a patrol car comes after you)   L: reset the police
  • M: world map with towns and landmarks   Esc / P: pause menu
  • Money: win races, score drifts (handbrake), J for a delivery job, lose the police
  • N: Crazy Taxi shift   U: eat at Mario's Pizza, Stack Shack, El Fuego or Best Kebap (+25% cash for 3 min)
  • Enter: start an event at a glowing marker (red = drag race, purple = drift) or take a food delivery order at a restaurant
  • Drag races: wait for green, then tap GEAR UP (Shift or E) as the rev bar hits the green zone for a perfect shift
  • Levels: everything you earn gives RP; each level pays a bonus and +3% on all payouts
  • Airport: your cargo plane - G near the back lowers the ramp, drive in, F to get out, I to sit in a seat, F at the flight deck stairs to fly it with your car inside; buy a hangar and small plane on the green pad (B)
  • Plane: F to board, W/S throttle, A/D bank, Up/Down arrows nose, Q/E rudder, Space brakes, Y autopilot
